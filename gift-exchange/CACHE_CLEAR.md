# How to Force Update on Mobile

## Method 1: Hard Refresh (Recommended)
**iOS Safari:**
- Tap and hold the refresh button
- Select "Reload Without Content Blockers" or just keep holding until it refreshes

**Android Chrome:**
- Tap the menu (3 dots)
- Tap "Settings" > "Privacy" > "Clear browsing data"
- Select "Cached images and files"
- Or: Tap and hold the refresh button

## Method 2: Clear Site Data
**iOS Safari:**
- Settings > Safari > Clear History and Website Data

**Android Chrome:**
- Settings > Site settings > All sites > Find your site > Clear & reset

## Method 3: Private/Incognito Mode
Open the page in a private/incognito window - this bypasses cache

## Method 4: Add ?clear=1 to URL
Add `?clear=1` to the end of the URL and refresh

## If Service Worker is Causing Issues:
1. Go to browser settings
2. Find "Site settings" or "Website data"
3. Find your site
4. Clear storage/data
5. Unregister service worker if shown
