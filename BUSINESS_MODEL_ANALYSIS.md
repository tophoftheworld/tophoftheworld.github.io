## 💡 Alternative Pricing Models

### **Option A: Per-Employee Pricing**
- **Base**: ₱500/month + ₱150/employee/month
- **Example**: 5 employees = ₱500 + (5 × ₱150) = ₱1,250/month
- **Pros**: Scales with business size
- **Cons**: Can get expensive for larger teams

### **Option B: Transaction-Based (for POS)**
- **Base**: ₱1,000/month
- **Transaction Fee**: ₱0.50 per transaction
- **Example**: 1,000 transactions/month = ₱1,000 + ₱500 = ₱1,500/month
- **Pros**: Pay for what you use
- **Cons**: Harder to predict costs

### **Option C: Freemium Model**
- **Free Tier**: 
  - Up to 3 employees
  - Basic attendance only
  - Limited to 1 month data retention
- **Paid Tiers**: As above
- **Pros**: Low barrier to entry, viral growth
- **Cons**: Higher support costs, conversion challenges
