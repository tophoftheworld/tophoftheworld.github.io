Finger Lab

Five webcam mini-apps powered by MediaPipe hand tracking. Allow camera access, hold your hand in frame, and play.

01

Air Paint 3D

Right-hand pinch draws in 3D — hand size in frame sets depth (small = far, large = near). Left hand rotates.

 02

Bubble Pop

Pop rising bubbles by touching them with any fingertip. Build a combo streak before time runs out.

 03

Finger Piano

Play a one-octave keyboard by hovering your index finger over the keys. Web Audio synth tones.

 04

Particle Magnet

Thousands of particles swirl around your fingertips. Pinch to flip between attract and repel.

 05

Gesture Match

Match the target gesture — fist, peace, open palm, or finger counts — before the timer expires.

Requires a webcam and a modern browser (Chrome, Edge, or Firefox). Processing runs locally in your browser via MediaPipe Hands. Good lighting and a plain background help tracking stay stable. The webcam is mirrored so hand overlays line up with your movement. Most experiments track both hands.
